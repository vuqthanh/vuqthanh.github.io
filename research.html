Research

Current Interests and Ongoing Projects

  • Powers of edge ideals and their homological invariants. Together with Nguyen Cong Minh, Le Dinh Nam, Thieu Dinh Phong, and Phan Thi Thuy, we developed a new approach toward understanding homological invariants of edge ideals of graphs.
  • Linearity defect of matrices of linear forms. With Hop Nguyen, I computed the linearity defect of ideals of minors of 2 × e matrices of linear forms.
  • Regularity of symbolic powers of monomial ideals. I am interested in explicit calculations of regularity.
  • Regularity and defining equations of monomial curves. I study bounds for regularity and defining equations.
