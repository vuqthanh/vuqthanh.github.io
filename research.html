Research Interests

I am currently working on several problems involving invariants of monomial and binomial ideals associated with graphs. For a recent list of publications, please see my arXiv page.

Publications

  1. Associated primes of the second power of closed neighborhood ideals of graphs, with H. T. T. Hien, Journal of Algebra and Its Applications (2026).
  2. Unmixed and sequentially Cohen-Macaulay skew tableau ideals, with D. T. Hoang, Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics 62 (2025), 26.
  3. Cochordal zero divisor graphs and Betti numbers of their edge ideals, with L. X. Dung, Communications in Algebra 54 (2026), 736–744.
  4. Betti numbers of powers of path ideals of cycles, with S. Balanescu and M. Cimpoeas, Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics 61 (2025), 44.
  5. Regularity of normal Rees algebras of edge ideals of graphs, with T. Q. Hoa and C. H. Linh, Journal of Algebra 680 (2025), 1-11.
  6. Depth and regularity of tableau ideals, with D. T. Hoang, Advances in Applied Mathematics 169 (2025), 102913.
